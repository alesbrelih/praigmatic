# Architectural Decisions

This directory records architectural and technical decisions made during development.

## Purpose

Decision records capture the context, rationale, and trade-offs behind significant choices.
They serve as a reference for future work and help new contributors understand why things
are the way they are.

## Format

Each decision is a markdown file with:

- **Title:** What was decided
- **Context:** What prompted the decision
- **Options:** Alternatives considered
- **Decision:** What was chosen and why
- **Trade-offs:** What was sacrificed
- **Date:** When the decision was made
